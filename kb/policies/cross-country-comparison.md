## Why this matters
Employees frequently compare notes with colleagues in other operating countries and reach the wrong
conclusion. The differences are substantial, not cosmetic.

## Annual leave
| Country | Entitlement | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Saudi Arabia | 21 days, rising to 30 after 5 years | Calendar days |
| UAE | 30 days flat after 1 year | Calendar days |
| Egypt | 15 days year one, 21 from year two, 30 at 10 years or age 50 | Working days |
| Jordan | 14 days, rising to 21 after 5 years | Working days |

## End of service
| Country | Employer-paid per-year gratuity? | Wage base | Reduced on resignation? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Saudi Arabia | Yes — 15 days per year for years 1-5, 30 thereafter | Wage including allowances | Yes, sharply |
| UAE | Yes — 21 days per year for years 1-5, 30 thereafter | Basic wage only | No |
| Egypt | No — provided through social insurance | n/a | n/a |
| Jordan | No — displaced by Social Security Corporation coverage | n/a | n/a |

The two most consequential differences: Saudi Arabia computes on wage **including allowances** while
the UAE uses **basic only**, and Saudi Arabia is the only one of the four that reduces the award when
an employee resigns.

## Probation maximum
| Country | Maximum | Extendable? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Saudi Arabia | 90 days | To 180 by written agreement |
| UAE | 6 months | No |
| Egypt | 3 months | No, and only once per employer |
| Jordan | 3 months | No mechanism in law |

## Sick leave
| Country | Structure |
| --- | --- |
| Saudi Arabia | 30 days full, 60 at 75%, 30 unpaid (per year) |
| UAE | 15 full, 30 half, 45 unpaid (90 per year); none during probation |
| Egypt (industrial) | 3 months full, 6 at 85%, 3 at 75% per three years of service |
| Jordan | 14 days full, plus 14 more at full pay if hospitalised |

Ask the agent for your own position. It applies the rule for your country of employment and your
length of service, and tells you where a figure is not yet confirmed.

## لماذا تهم هذه المقارنة
تختلف الاستحقاقات بين دول التشغيل اختلافاً جوهرياً، وأهم فرقين: تحتسب السعودية المكافأة على الأجر شاملاً البدلات بينما تحتسبها الإمارات على الأجر الأساسي فقط، والسعودية هي الوحيدة من الأربع التي تخفض المكافأة عند الاستقالة. ولا يوجد في مصر والأردن بدل نهاية خدمة يدفعه صاحب العمل عن كل سنة، إذ تتم التسوية عبر التأمينات والضمان الاجتماعي.
