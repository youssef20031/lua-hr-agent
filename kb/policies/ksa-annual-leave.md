## Entitlement
Under **Article 109 of the Saudi Labour Law**:

- **21 days** per year for employees with less than five years of continuous service.
- **30 days** per year once the employee completes **five continuous years** with the employer.

The step-up is based on continuous service with this employer. Service with a previous employer does
not count.

## How it is counted
Calendar days.

## Practical points
- The employer schedules leave according to business needs, but must let you take your entitlement.
- Unused leave is paid on termination.
- Ask the agent for your own balance rather than assuming: it reflects days already taken and any
  request still awaiting approval.

## الاستحقاق
بموجب **المادة ١٠٩ من نظام العمل السعودي**:
- **٢١ يوماً** سنوياً لمن تقل خدمته المتصلة عن خمس سنوات.
- **٣٠ يوماً** سنوياً لمن أتم **خمس سنوات متصلة** لدى صاحب العمل.

وتُحتسب بالأيام التقويمية، ويُدفع بدل الرصيد غير المستخدم عند انتهاء الخدمة.
