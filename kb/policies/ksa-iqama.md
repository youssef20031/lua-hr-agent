## What the Iqama is
The Iqama is the residence permit that gives a non-Saudi employee legal residency in Saudi Arabia.
It is issued under the employer sponsorship and is tied to your employment. It is your primary
identity document in the Kingdom.

## Validity and renewal
Typically issued for one year and renewed annually, though longer terms are issued in some cases.
The employer is responsible for renewing it and for paying the associated fees and levies. You are
responsible for keeping a valid passport and current medical insurance so that renewal can proceed.

Renewal begins **90 days** before expiry, with reminders at 90, 60, 30 and 7 days.

## Why expiry is serious
An expired Iqama is not a paperwork inconvenience:

- You lose legal residency status.
- You cannot travel, and you should not attempt to leave or re-enter the country.
- Banking, tenancy, vehicle registration, medical services and mobile contracts all depend on a valid
  Iqama and may be blocked.
- The employer incurs fines, and repeated expiry affects the company Nitaqat standing, which in turn
  affects everyone visas and renewals.

## What to do
Respond to the first renewal reminder. If your passport expires within six months, renew it at your
embassy immediately and give Government Relations the new number. If your Iqama has already expired,
contact Government Relations the same day.

## ما هي الإقامة
الإقامة هي رخصة الإقامة النظامية للموظف غير السعودي في المملكة، وتصدر تحت كفالة صاحب العمل وترتبط بعلاقة العمل، وهي وثيقة هويتك الأساسية.

## لماذا انتهاؤها أمر خطير
تفقد صفة الإقامة النظامية، ولا يمكنك السفر، وقد تتعطل الخدمات البنكية والسكن والخدمات الطبية والاتصالات، وتتحمل المنشأة غرامات تؤثر على نطاقها وبالتالي على تأشيرات جميع الموظفين.
