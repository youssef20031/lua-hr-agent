## What Nitaqat is
Nitaqat is the Saudi Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development programme that measures how
well an employer meets its Saudization target — the required proportion of Saudi nationals in its
workforce. The target varies by sector and by company size, so two employers of different sizes in
the same sector face different thresholds.

## The bands
Employers are placed in colour bands reflecting performance against the target, from **Platinum**
(highest) through **Green** tiers, down to **Red** (non-compliant).

## Why the band matters
An employer band directly affects day-to-day HR operations:

- **Work visa issuance** — higher bands can obtain new block visas; low bands cannot.
- **Iqama renewals** — renewal can be restricted for employers in the lowest bands.
- **Employee transfers** — an employee may be able to transfer sponsorship away from a Red-band
  employer without that employer consent, and higher-band employers can recruit from lower-band ones
  more freely.
- **Government services** — access to a range of ministry services is tied to the band.

## What this means for you
If you are told that a visa, an Iqama renewal or a transfer is delayed for compliance reasons, the
band is usually why. Government Relations can tell you the current position. Nothing about the band
changes your individual contractual entitlements.

## ما هو نطاقات
برنامج لوزارة الموارد البشرية والتنمية الاجتماعية يقيس مدى التزام المنشأة بنسب التوطين المطلوبة، وتختلف النسبة المستهدفة حسب النشاط وحجم المنشأة.

## أهمية النطاق
يؤثر نطاق المنشأة على إصدار تأشيرات العمل، وتجديد الإقامات، وإمكانية نقل الكفالة، والوصول إلى الخدمات الحكومية. ولا يؤثر النطاق على استحقاقاتك التعاقدية الفردية.
