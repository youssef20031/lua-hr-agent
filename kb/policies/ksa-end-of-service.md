## How it accrues — Article 84
- **Half a month wage** for each of the first five years of service.
- **A full month wage** for each year thereafter.
- A proportional amount for any part of a year.

## Which wage
The **last wage**, which under the Labour Law means basic wage **plus regular allowances** — not
basic salary alone. Using basic alone understates the award, often substantially.

## Resignation reduces the award — Article 85
| Continuous service | Proportion payable on resignation |
| --- | --- |
| Under 2 years | Nothing |
| 2 to under 5 years | One third |
| 5 to under 10 years | Two thirds |
| 10 years or more | Full award |

Termination by the employer, expiry of a fixed-term contract, death and total disability all pay the
**full** award. Only resignation is reduced.

## Worked example
An employee on 12,000 SAR a month, hired 1 March 2018, leaving 1 March 2026 — eight years of service.

- Daily wage: 12,000 ÷ 30 = 400 SAR
- First five years: 5 × 15 days = 75 days = 30,000 SAR
- Remaining 3.005 years: 3.005 × 30 days = 90.16 days = 36,065.75 SAR
- Gross award: **66,065.75 SAR**
- On resignation at eight years, two thirds applies: **44,043.84 SAR**

Ask the agent to calculate your own figure. It reads your hire date and wage from the HRIS and shows
the full working.

## طريقة الاحتساب — المادة ٨٤
- **نصف شهر** عن كل سنة من السنوات الخمس الأولى.
- **شهر كامل** عن كل سنة بعد ذلك.
- ويُحتسب جزء السنة بنسبته.

وتُحسب المكافأة على **الأجر الأخير** شاملاً البدلات المنتظمة، وليس الراتب الأساسي وحده.

## أثر الاستقالة — المادة ٨٥
أقل من سنتين: لا شيء. من سنتين إلى أقل من خمس: الثلث. من خمس إلى أقل من عشر: الثلثان. عشر سنوات فأكثر: المكافأة كاملة.
