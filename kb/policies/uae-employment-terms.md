Governed by **Federal Decree-Law 33/2021**, as amended in 2022, 2023 and 2024.

## Annual leave — Article 29
- **30 days** per year after one year of service. There is no seniority escalator; it is flat.
- **2 days per month** for service between six and twelve months.
- **Calendar days**: weekends and public holidays falling inside the leave count as part of it.
- Untaken leave is paid on exit regardless of length of service.

## End of service gratuity — Article 51
- **21 days of basic wage** per year for the first five years.
- **30 days of basic wage** per year thereafter.
- Minimum **one year** of continuous service to qualify.
- Computed on the **last basic wage only** — housing, transport and similar allowances are excluded.
  This is the opposite of the Saudi position and the most common cross-border error.
- Capped at two years wage. In practice this binds only at around 26 years of service.
- **Resignation does not reduce the award.** The one-third and two-thirds tiers people remember were
  in the former Federal Law 8/1980, which this law repealed.
- Dismissal under Article 44 does not forfeit the gratuity either.
- All entitlements are payable within **14 days** of the contract ending (Article 53).

## Probation — Article 9
- **Six months** maximum, not extendable, and only once with the same employer.
- The employer must give at least **14 days** written notice to terminate during probation.
- An employee moving to another UAE employer must give **one month** notice.

## Sick leave — Article 31
- **90 days** per year in total, consecutive or intermittent: first 15 at full pay, next 30 at half
  pay, remaining 45 unpaid.
- **No paid sick leave during probation.**
- Notify within three business days with a Medical Authority report.

## ملخص
الإجازة السنوية ٣٠ يوماً تقويمياً بعد سنة من الخدمة. ومكافأة نهاية الخدمة ٢١ يوماً من **الأجر الأساسي** عن كل سنة من السنوات الخمس الأولى ثم ٣٠ يوماً عن كل سنة بعدها، بحد أدنى سنة خدمة، ولا تُخفَّض عند الاستقالة. وفترة التجربة ستة أشهر غير قابلة للتمديد. والإجازة المرضية ٩٠ يوماً: ١٥ بأجر كامل ثم ٣٠ بنصف أجر ثم ٤٥ بدون أجر.
