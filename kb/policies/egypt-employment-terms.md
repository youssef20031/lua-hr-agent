## Which law applies
**Labour Law No. 14 of 2025**, published 3 May 2025 and in force from **1 September 2025**. It
repealed Law 12/2003. Any guidance still citing Law 12/2003 is out of date.

## Annual leave — Article 124
| Service or status | Entitlement |
| --- | --- |
| First year | **15 days** |
| From the second year | **21 days** |
| 10 years service, or age over 50 | **30 days** |
| Persons with disabilities and persons of short stature | **45 days** |

Note the first-year figure. It is 15 days, not 21 — this changed with the new law.

Official holidays and weekly rest days are **excluded** from the count, so these are effectively
working days. Accrual in the first year is pro-rata, subject to a six-month qualifying period.
Hazardous work or remote areas add seven days.

Under Article 125 you must take at least 15 days a year including one continuous week, and leave
balance is settled for a maximum of three years.

## End of service — Article 172
**There is no Gulf-style employer-paid gratuity accruing per year of service.** Article 172 provides
a gratuity only where the social insurance system does not apply, or for the portion of wage falling
outside the insurance contribution wage. For a normally-insured employee, the employer owes no
per-year gratuity.

What you receive instead:
- Your social insurance entitlement.
- Payment for accrued annual leave.
- Where a dismissal is for an **unlawful cause**, Article 165 gives damages of **not less than two
  months wage per year of service**. This is compensation for unlawful termination, not general
  severance — a distinction many English summaries blur.

All entitlements must be paid within **seven working days** of the relationship ending (Article 171).

## Probation — Article 90
**Three months** maximum, and an employee may **not** be placed under probation more than once with
the same employer.

## Sick leave — Article 131
For **industrial establishments** under Law 15/2017 — which includes this employer plant operations
— the entitlement per three years of service is 3 months at full pay, then 6 months at 85%, then 3
months at 75%. For other staff the general rule applies: duration set by the competent medical
authority, with pay governed by the Social Insurance and Pensions Law 148/2019. Whatever social
insurance pays is deducted from the employer liability.

## ملخص
يسري **قانون العمل رقم ١٤ لسنة ٢٠٢٥** اعتباراً من ١ سبتمبر ٢٠٢٥ وقد ألغى القانون ١٢ لسنة ٢٠٠٣.
الإجازة السنوية ١٥ يوماً في السنة الأولى ثم ٢١ يوماً، و٣٠ يوماً لمن أمضى عشر سنوات أو تجاوز الخمسين.
ولا يوجد بدل نهاية خدمة يدفعه صاحب العمل عن كل سنة، إذ تتم التسوية عبر التأمينات الاجتماعية، ويستحق التعويض عن الفصل لسبب غير مشروع بما لا يقل عن أجر شهرين عن كل سنة خدمة.
فترة الاختبار ثلاثة أشهر ولا يجوز تكرارها لدى صاحب العمل نفسه.
