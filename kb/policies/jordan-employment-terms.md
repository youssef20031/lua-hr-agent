Governed by **Labour Law No. 8 of 1996**, as amended to 2023.

## Annual leave — Article 61
- **14 days** per year.
- **21 days** after five consecutive years with the same employer.
- Official holidays, religious holidays and weekends are **excluded** from the count.
- Carry-over is permitted to the following year only, and lapses after that.
- Leave may be split, in blocks of not less than two days.

## End of service — Article 32 and the Social Security Corporation
**For a normal private-sector employee, no employer-paid gratuity is owed on leaving — whether you
resign or are terminated.**

Article 32 provides one month wage per year of service, but it opens with a carve-out: it applies
only to a worker **not** subject to the Social Security Law. SSC coverage is compulsory for all
workers subject to the Labour Law aged 16 or over, regardless of contract type, at any employer with
one or more workers. The residual class is narrow — mainly irregular workers engaged fewer than 16
days a month.

What you receive instead is your SSC entitlement: an old-age pension or lump-sum compensation, plus
your unemployment-insurance savings balance.

## Arbitrary dismissal — Article 25
If a court finds within sixty days that a termination was arbitrary, it may order reinstatement or
compensation of **half a month wage per year of service, with a floor of two months wages**,
computed on the last wage, in addition to notice allowance.

The "three to six months" figure still widely quoted is the **superseded** pre-2019 value, replaced
by Law No. 14 of 2019.

## Probation — Article 35
**Three months** maximum, with no extension or renewal mechanism in the law at all. Termination
during probation requires no notice and pays no compensation. If you are kept on past probation the
contract becomes indefinite-term and the probation counts towards total service.

## Sick leave — Article 65
- **14 days** at full pay, on a report from a physician approved by the firm.
- A further **14 days at full pay** where the worker is an inpatient.
- There is **no reduced-pay tier** — both blocks are full pay, contrary to a common misconception.
- In establishments of 20 or more workers the medical report must be approved by a medical committee.

## ملخص
الإجازة السنوية ١٤ يوماً وترتفع إلى ٢١ يوماً بعد خمس سنوات متصلة لدى صاحب العمل نفسه.
ولا يستحق الموظف المشمول بالضمان الاجتماعي بدل نهاية خدمة من صاحب العمل، إذ تنطبق المادة ٣٢ فقط على غير الخاضعين لقانون الضمان، والشمول إلزامي لجميع العاملين تقريباً.
وفي حالة الفصل التعسفي تنص المادة ٢٥ على نصف أجر شهر عن كل سنة خدمة بحد أدنى أجر شهرين.
فترة التجربة ثلاثة أشهر دون آلية تمديد. والإجازة المرضية ١٤ يوماً بأجر كامل يضاف إليها ١٤ يوماً بأجر كامل عند التنويم.
