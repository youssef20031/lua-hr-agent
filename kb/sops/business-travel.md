## Purpose
How to obtain approval and arrange travel for a business trip.

## Steps
1. Submit a travel request at least **10 working days** before departure, stating the purpose,
   destination, dates and estimated cost.
2. Your line manager approves. Trips above the published threshold also need Division Director
   approval.
3. The Travel Desk books flights and accommodation. Personal bookings are not reimbursed.
4. For international travel, confirm your visa and, in Saudi Arabia, your exit and re-entry visa.
5. Submit your expense claim within 30 days of returning.

## Class of travel
By grade, as published in the Travel Policy. Flights under four hours are economy class for all
grades.

## Per diem
Paid at the published rate for the destination city, covering meals and incidental expenses. Where a
meal is provided by the host or included in the hotel rate, the per diem is reduced accordingly.

## الغرض
كيفية الحصول على الاعتماد وترتيب السفر في مهمة عمل.

## الخطوات
1. قدّم طلب السفر قبل **١٠ أيام عمل** على الأقل من المغادرة.
2. اعتماد المدير المباشر، وللرحلات التي تتجاوز الحد المنشور يلزم اعتماد مدير القطاع.
3. يتولى مكتب السفر حجز الطيران والإقامة، والحجوزات الشخصية غير قابلة للاسترداد.
4. للسفر الدولي تأكد من التأشيرة، وفي السعودية من تأشيرة الخروج والعودة.
