## Purpose
How to claim reimbursement for business expenses.

## Deadline
Claims must be submitted within **30 days** of the expense being incurred. Claims older than 60 days
require a written exception from the Finance Director and are normally refused.

## Steps
1. Gather original receipts. Photographs are accepted if legible and showing the full receipt.
2. Submit the claim with the business purpose and the cost centre.
3. Your line manager approves within five working days.
4. Finance Shared Services validates and pays with the next payroll run.

## What is reimbursable
- Business travel: flights, accommodation, ground transport, and per diem at the published rate.
- Client and supplier hospitality, with the names and companies of attendees recorded.
- Mileage in a personal vehicle, at the published rate per kilometre.

## What is not reimbursable
- Personal expenses, fines, and traffic violations.
- Upgrades to travel class beyond your grade entitlement.
- Expenses already covered by per diem.

## الغرض
كيفية المطالبة باسترداد مصروفات العمل.

## الموعد النهائي
يجب تقديم المطالبة خلال **٣٠ يوماً** من تاريخ المصروف، والمطالبات التي تتجاوز ٦٠ يوماً تتطلب استثناءً خطياً وعادة تُرفض.

## غير قابل للاسترداد
المصروفات الشخصية والغرامات ومخالفات المرور، وترقية درجة السفر بما يتجاوز الاستحقاق.
