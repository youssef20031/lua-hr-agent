## Purpose
The process for resigning and receiving your final settlement.

## Notice period
As stated in your contract, normally **30 days** for non-managerial roles and **60 days** for
managerial roles. Notice runs from the date HR acknowledges your written resignation, not from the
date you told your manager.

## Steps
1. Submit a written resignation to your line manager, copying your HR Business Partner. State your
   intended last working day.
2. HR acknowledges in writing and confirms the notice end date.
3. Complete the handover document with your manager.
4. Complete the clearance form: IT equipment, access cards, company vehicle, advances, and any
   outstanding expense claims.
5. Attend an exit interview with your HR Business Partner.
6. Final settlement is paid within the timeframe required by local law after your last working day
   and completion of clearance.

## What the final settlement includes
- Salary to your last working day.
- Payment for accrued but untaken annual leave.
- End-of-service entitlement, where the country of employment provides one. This differs sharply by
  country and by whether you resign or are terminated — ask the agent to calculate yours.
- Less any advances, unreturned assets or notice shortfall.

## Important
Do not assume an end-of-service figure from a colleague in another country. Saudi Arabia and the UAE
pay a per-year gratuity on very different bases; Egypt and Jordan provide for end of service through
social insurance instead. Ask the agent for your own position.

## Egypt: an additional legal step
Under Labour Law 14/2025 a resignation must be in writing and **ratified by the competent labour
office**. Service does not end until the resignation is accepted, and you may withdraw it within ten
days of being notified of acceptance.

## الغرض
إجراءات تقديم الاستقالة واستلام المخالصة النهائية.

## فترة الإشعار
حسب العقد، وعادة **٣٠ يوماً** للوظائف غير الإشرافية و**٦٠ يوماً** للوظائف الإشرافية، وتبدأ من تاريخ إقرار الموارد البشرية باستلام الاستقالة الخطية.

## تشمل المخالصة النهائية
- الراتب حتى آخر يوم عمل.
- بدل رصيد الإجازات السنوية غير المستخدمة.
- مستحقات نهاية الخدمة، حيثما تنص أنظمة دولة العمل على ذلك، وهي تختلف اختلافاً كبيراً بين الدول.

## في مصر
يجب أن تكون الاستقالة مكتوبة ومصدقة من مكتب العمل المختص، ولا تنتهي الخدمة إلا بقبولها، ويجوز سحبها خلال عشرة أيام من الإخطار بالقبول.
