## Purpose
How housing allowance is calculated and paid.

## Entitlement
Housing allowance is **three months of basic salary per year**, equivalent to 25% of annual basic
salary, unless your contract states otherwise. Employees in company-provided accommodation do not
receive the allowance.

## Payment options
1. **Monthly** — added to your monthly payroll by default.
2. **Annual advance** — paid as a lump sum at the start of the lease year, on request, to cover a
   landlord demanding a year in advance.

## Requesting the annual advance
1. Submit a copy of the signed lease contract, in the employee own name.
2. Compensation and Benefits verifies the lease value and dates.
3. The advance is paid within **ten working days** of approval.
4. If you leave the company during the lease year, the unearned portion is recovered from your final
   settlement.

## Notes
- The allowance is paid on basic salary, not total package.
- Leases in a spouse or relative name are not accepted for the advance.

## الغرض
كيفية احتساب بدل السكن وصرفه.

## الاستحقاق
بدل السكن يعادل **ثلاثة أشهر من الراتب الأساسي سنوياً** أي ٢٥٪ من الراتب الأساسي السنوي، ما لم ينص العقد على خلاف ذلك.

## خيارات الصرف
1. **شهرياً** ضمن الراتب، وهو الخيار الافتراضي.
2. **دفعة سنوية مقدمة** عند الطلب، لتغطية إيجار يطلبه المالك مقدماً.
