## Purpose
How your Iqama (residence permit) is renewed, and what you must provide.

## Timing
Government Relations begins the renewal **90 days** before expiry. You will receive automated
reminders at 90, 60, 30 and 7 days. Renewal cannot be completed in the last few days before expiry
if documents are missing, so respond to the first reminder.

## What you must provide
1. A passport valid for at least **six months** beyond the renewal date. This is the single most
   common cause of delay.
2. A valid medical insurance certificate. Government Relations renews this in parallel.
3. Updated contact details and current address.

## Steps
1. Respond to the 90-day reminder confirming your passport validity.
2. If your passport expires within six months, renew it at your embassy first and give Government
   Relations the new number.
3. Government Relations pays the renewal fees and levies through the company account.
4. The renewed Iqama is issued electronically through Absher and Muqeem.
5. You receive confirmation and an updated Iqama card where a physical card is issued.

## If your Iqama has already expired
Contact Government Relations the same day. Do not travel, and do not use the expired Iqama for any
transaction. Late renewal attracts a fine payable by the employer, and repeated expiry affects the
company compliance standing.

## الغرض
كيفية تجديد الإقامة وما هي المستندات المطلوبة منك.

## التوقيت
تبدأ إدارة العلاقات الحكومية إجراءات التجديد قبل **٩٠ يوماً** من انتهاء الصلاحية، وستصلك تذكيرات آلية عند ٩٠ و٦٠ و٣٠ و٧ أيام.

## المستندات المطلوبة
1. جواز سفر صالح لمدة **ستة أشهر** على الأقل بعد تاريخ التجديد.
2. شهادة تأمين طبي سارية.
3. بيانات اتصال وعنوان محدّثان.

## إذا انتهت إقامتك
تواصل مع إدارة العلاقات الحكومية في اليوم نفسه، ولا تسافر ولا تستخدم الإقامة المنتهية في أي معاملة.
